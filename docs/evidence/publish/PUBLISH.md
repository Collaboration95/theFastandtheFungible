## Phone publishing

Enable `WEB_PUBLISHING=on` in the engine and publisher, with the same private `OPERATOR_TOKEN` (at least 24 characters). Default: off. A note is pending until the operator approves it in Writers → Operator review. `AUTO_APPROVE=on` (default off) approves pre-checked notes after `AUTO_APPROVE_SECONDS` (default 5, range 1–3600). Keep auto approval off for public unattended use.

The form accepts 150–3,000 words, at least four paragraphs, a 3–100 character title, 1–8 topics, a price from S$0.01 through the existing per-source cap (S$1.00), and an optional display name. Public metadata cannot share eight consecutive words with the paid body. Prechecks reject common secrets, XRPL seeds, checksum-valid BIP39 recovery phrases of 12–24 words, identity/payment numbers, contact details, repeated text, common spam, active HTML, more than three links, and normalized duplicate bodies. These heuristics assist human moderation; they cannot establish factual accuracy, consent or universal PII removal.

Queue, rate limits, status capabilities and private signing material are persisted beside the publisher journal in `web-publishing.db` (0600). There are at most 1,000 retained submissions and ten attempts per minute globally. Operator requests use `X-Operator-Token`; tokens are compared in constant time, retained only in operator UI memory and never logged. Private status uses a random capability saved in the writer's browser. A bookmarkable private link carries it in the URL fragment, which is not sent in HTTP requests; the status request carries it in a header. Neither public status nor search returns body text or signing material. Failed publishing preserves its key and can be retried by the operator. Restart restores live notes and returns interrupted approvals to pending.

New accounts use the existing Testnet funding helper. Pasted payout addresses require a matching operator-held signing key, configured privately as an `XRPL_PUBLISHER_*_SEED`. An arbitrary unsigned address cannot meet the existing wallet-bound manifest contract. The UI explains this before submission; browser seeds are rejected. Accounts created by the service are custodial Testnet payout accounts.

## Enforced credential mode

The two regulated flags accept literal `on`, `off` or an empty unset value; misspelled values fail startup rather than being normalized inconsistently across payment boundaries.

`REGULATED_MODE=on` (default off) makes purchase policy require a fresh accepted XRPL Credential under `WRITER_CREDENTIAL_DOMAIN`. The checked wallet must be the wallet bound to the signed manifest. Enforcement also rejects a validated ledger older than 120 seconds or more than 30 seconds in the future. There is no advisory badge override: missing, unaccepted, expired, revoked, malformed, wrong-domain, pending or failed reads refuse. The policy records the refusal before quote/reservation/signing; the final submission transition and durable pre-broadcast write also require the same payee/domain admission within ten seconds. A prepared unsigned escrow is rechecked after restart, including during reconciliation; already signed recovery retains its original identity. Tests cover both accepted and revoked restart cases and admission expiry/domain changes before broadcast. Regulation leaves the existing budget, pause, signature, intent and proof checks in force. An offline fixture comparison exercises 32 purchase-manager cases and 256 admission cases (64 accepted and 192 refused credential observations); regulation cannot increase spending or grants.

`PUBLISHER_REGULATED_MODE=on` (default off) independently requires an accepted buyer credential before escrow quotes and before either paid GET or facilitator settlement. Direct facilitator access cannot bypass the check. Proven prior settlement can still recover delivery without authorizing another charge. Royalty payouts also require a fresh accepted writer credential; refused unsigned royalties follow the existing return-to-caller path. Every refusal is a hash-chained audit event with a plain reason and no secret or body data.

Ordinary offline app builds consume the committed artifact and its generated TypeScript projection; tests require both representations to match. Runtime schema and hash validation fail closed on tampering or a malformed snapshot. Regulated mode adds this exact-address screen to the existing operator denylist. An address absent from this dated list is not a general clearance of the payee or a legal compliance claim.
