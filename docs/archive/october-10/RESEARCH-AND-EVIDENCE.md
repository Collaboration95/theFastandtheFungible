# October 10: research and evidence implementation plan

Planning baseline: October 2, 2026; all implementation and verification remain proposed. [INTERFACES.md](INTERFACES.md) controls architecture. Preserve React/Express/TypeScript, existing Zod/Vitest/Playwright, the single data-centre domain, approximately twelve synthetic resources, and one local HTTP publisher with three profiles. Primary demonstration: live model decisions and synthesis, local publisher transport, simulated settlement. Model rehearsal/fallback and settlement mode are separate, visible fields.

Sequence: RES-01 contracts and corpus first; RES-02 transition core alongside COM-01..04; RES-03 baseline/final integration; VER-01 exercises the resulting API. Integrate a minimal complete flow by October 4; complete variants/recovery October 5–6, integration/readability October 7, and release/rehearsal October 8–9. The index owns sequencing. ## RES-01 — Versioned corpus, provenance, and protected evidence boundary

**Priority: P0. Outcome / 80–20 reason:** a small, credible corpus separates discovery promises from readable evidence and distinguishes repeated coverage from independent corroboration. This proves purchase restraint and citation correctness.

**Current-code anchors:** `data/mock-articles.json` has twelve records, four premium resources, issuer derivatives, supplier reporting, and grid reporting. `server/catalog.ts` validates dates/licenses/unique IDs, but requires ID suffixes and `xrpDrops = priceCents × 1,000`; `toSource` uses an exclusion spread. `server/index.ts:sourceType` infers categories from named families. `src/domain.ts:tfidfScore` computes query-token overlap, not TF-IDF; `rankSources` also trusts precomputed relevance/gap/novelty. `tests/domain.test.ts` checks named canonical ordering rather than identity invariance.

**Dependencies:** the shared interfaces fix resource/version/digest and grant shapes; COM-02/04 consume the RES-01 definitions. This issue does not wait for those implementations. VER-01 owns execution of curated cases.

**Ordered subtasks:**

- [ ] RES-01.a Introduce explicit public-resource, protected-content, accessible-span, and private-evaluation types. Use positive allowlist serializers in catalog/API/model packets; eliminate spreading complete catalog records into public output.
- [ ] RES-01.b Add `publisherProfileId`, explicit `sourceType`, `resourceId`, `resourceVersion`, provenance origin/derivation references, and stable `familyId`. Keep display publisher/title independent of identity. Assign the corpus to `public-records`, `supplier-wire`, and `grid-research` using the exact seed mapping in INTERFACES; profiles may contain multiple display publications.
- [ ] RES-01.c Give spans an owner tuple, content digest, and body offsets. Validate exact substring/offset correspondence, resource/version uniqueness, known derivation references, and acyclic derivation. Set derivative families to their underlying origin. Split the current generic `family-context` where cooling, financing, construction, and packaging have independent origins; thematic similarity alone does not establish shared provenance.
- [ ] RES-01.d Curate bounded demand, equipment-delivery, and grid/energisation questions. Keep previews descriptive; remove outcome-bearing span labels such as “Changed conclusion.” Create replacement-version variants for open sufficiency, unchanged support, and contradiction, plus identity/price transformations; retain roughly twelve resources per scenario.
- [ ] RES-01.e Rename `tfidfScore` to `lexicalOverlapScore`, update callers/events/method labels, and report `LEXICAL_METADATA_HEURISTIC`. Remove “semantic retrieval” claims. Rank using overlap with question/active-gap text, public topic tags, originality, and provenance diversity; curated relevance/gap/novelty fields remain import/evaluation data, excluded from production decision packets and ranking.

**Contracts / failure cases:** public discovery contains identity/version, publisher/title/date/type, preview, access tier, topic tags, provenance/family, license, and indicative price in explicit minor units/currency/mode. Indicative price is never an approval quote. Protected payload contains body, spans, and manifest digest; only COM-04's verified version grant makes premium spans accessible. Open-body loading also crosses COM-02's publisher seam. Span identity is `(resourceId, resourceVersion, spanId)`; names and `-open` suffixes cannot authorize reading.

Private evaluation records join those tuples to allowed propositions, required qualifications, contradictory propositions, and expected decisions. They reside in a separate server/test-only file, never imported into client code or production planner/synthesizer. Locked body, span text/labels, private labels, expected impact, and hidden relevance scores must be absent from previews, decisions, SSE, error payloads, and model requests. Public provenance may describe sourcing without certifying that a hidden claim is true. Bad schema/provenance fails startup or rejects publisher delivery; missing content does not become an empty successful evidence read. Remove currency conversion validation; COM owns separately denominated settlement units.

**Observable acceptance:** renaming/rekeying resources with corresponding references preserves decisions and family counts; a renamed derivative still contributes no new family. Independent context origins count separately. No serializer reveals a locked sentinel or privileged label before grant. Delivery of another version cannot supply citations for this version. Ranking contains no publisher/source-name special cases and is described accurately.

**Verification / evidence:** existing `tests/domain.test.ts` and `tests/e2e.spec.ts` provide ranking/catalog and premium-protection starting points. Extend them; propose `tests/evidence-contract.test.ts` for serializer leak scans, provenance, version/offset errors, and permutation invariance. Capture public/model packet samples and private rubric inventory separately, mutation results, and exact commands/exit statuses.

**Scope / done:** in: corpus curation, schemas, serializers, honest lexical retrieval, evaluation labels. Out: HTTP publisher implementation, settlement, storage tables, broad deduplication or arbitrary-source provenance inference. Done when the validated seed and replacement variants share COM's resource contract and all boundary/identity negatives pass.

## RES-02 — Bounded server research decisions and checkpoints

**Priority: P0. Outcome / 80–20 reason:** the server reads accessible evidence, names a material gap, proposes one useful purchase or stops, and resumes safely after approval/delivery. A reducer and checkpoint provide bounded agency.

**Current-code anchors:** `src/research-plan.ts` defines reviewable approaches and stop prose; these do not execute safeguards. `server/index.ts:advance` walks fixed phases and installs the same gap. `/purchase-decisions` and `server/llm.ts:planPurchase` exclude `circuit-note`, hardcode a 100-cent ceiling, and require choosing premium evidence. Purchasing named sources rewrites the thesis. `Run.version` exists but does not gate asynchronous updates; `tokenLimit` is presented without accounting. Existing scope matching accepts almost any data-centre question.

**Dependencies:** RES-01; COM-01's transactional checkpoint/revision seam; COM-02 quotes/discovery; COM-03 approval/intent status; COM-04 version grants. RES-03 supplies validated baseline/final artifacts. UI receives projections, never a second execution policy.

**Ordered subtasks:**

- [ ] RES-02.a Keep approach presets as priorities, but compile three supported facet IDs (`demand`, `equipment-delivery`, `grid-energisation`) and the 2028 horizon into executable requirements. Unknown domain/horizon returns scope limitation; freeform edited stop prose is explanatory, never executable permission.
- [ ] RES-02.b Extract a research reducer and a single transition executor from `advance`. Route legacy discover/rank/gaps/step/synthesize commands through the same guards; reject unknown/out-of-order actions. Browser commands include `expectedRevision` and `commandId`.
- [ ] RES-02.c Discover only approved profile/type metadata, read each allowed open resource once, then obtain a validated baseline before proposing any quote. Build facet coverage with supporting span references, explicit unknowns, and material-gap reasons from accessible content, not catalog answer keys.
- [ ] RES-02.d Replace the purchase-only schema with a strict Zod union: `CONTINUE` (unread accessible resource), `PROPOSE_PURCHASE` (one resource/version, gap facet, expected contribution), `STOP` (bounded answer/remaining uncertainty), or `ABSTAIN` (unsupported/insufficient evidence). Server computes eligibility before the call and rechecks afterward.
- [ ] RES-02.e Checkpoint every committed transition; implement revision conflict handling, approval rejection, limits, timeout/fallback, and delivery continuation. Remove source-specific thesis/gap mutations from the purchase endpoint; commerce status changes only schedule evidence reassessment.

**State contract:** `DRAFT → DISCOVERING → READING_OPEN → BASELINE → ASSESSING`. Assessment selects another accessible read, `AWAITING_APPROVAL`, `FINALIZING → COMPLETE` with outcome `STOP`, or terminal `ABSTAINED`. Accepted approval moves to `AWAITING_DELIVERY`; only a verified COM grant returns to `ASSESSING`. Payment pending/uncertain stays waiting with COM's recovery action. `PAUSED`, `CANCELLED`, and `BLOCKED` carry the saved continuation/reason. Rejected approval excludes that resource/version for this run and reassesses; no replacement is bought automatically. Pausing/cancelling research cannot erase or reverse an already submitted commerce intent.

Eligibility requires scope/type/profile membership, current resource version, explicit material gap, no existing grant/pending intent/rejection, supported license/mode, price within configurable ceiling and settled-plus-reserved budget, and an expected contribution beyond evidence already read. Derivative coverage with no new public sourcing/contribution is `REDUNDANT`, independent of name. Same-family sourcing is not automatically useless if public metadata describes a distinct contribution. Changed quote terms invalidate the proposal and require COM's fresh exact approval; the model cannot supply authoritative prices or approve anything.

`STOP/OPEN_SUFFICIENT` requires grounded coverage of the selected facets and no identified decision-material unknown; require two distinct provenance families overall for the canonical sustainability question. A qualified partial answer may `STOP/LIMITED_EVIDENCE` with named unresolved facets when purchases are declined/unaffordable/unnecessary for the explicitly narrowed conclusion. No readable relevant evidence yields `ABSTAIN`, not a fixed thesis. Coverage references validate structurally at runtime; semantic sufficiency receives the independent curated gate in VER-01. Neither family count nor a model's “sufficient” string alone proves closure.

**Bounds / checkpoint:** maximum twelve discovered resources, twenty-four committed research transitions, eight provider calls including synthesis, two purchase proposals, and one pending intent. Effective token cap is `min(requested tokenLimit, 64,000)`; permit zero purchase budget. Reserve input UTF-8 byte count plus output-token cap before calls as conservative token accounting, replace with reported usage when available, otherwise retain reservation labelled estimated. Bound decision output to 800 tokens, synthesis to 2,400, decision timeout to 30 seconds and synthesis to 45; oversized packets block explicitly, never truncate evidence silently. Limits stop with a supported partial answer or abstention. Invalid output permits one corrected attempt within the same limits, then `BLOCKED`; no retry creates approval. Offline fallback requires a deliberately selected fresh fixture run under INTERFACES.

Checkpoint fields: schema version, run/revision, immutable config/catalog snapshot identity, state/continuation, read owner tuples, facet support/gaps, baseline/final IDs, last validated decision, declined versions, quote/intent references, transition/call/token counters, selected model/settlement modes, reason/safe action. COM-01 atomically persists checkpoint plus event. Persist call reservation first; apply response only if revision/state still match. Startup exposes interrupted nonfinancial transitions for explicit resume; waiting intents reconcile through COM. Use one per-run in-process executor plus COM compare-and-swap, not a queue. Duplicate commands return committed results; stale commands return 409. SSE follows commit.

**Acceptance / verification:** open-sufficient case stops with zero intents; empty/unsupported scope abstains; unaffordable/redundant/unknown/already-granted/version-stale proposals are rejected. Price increase changes eligibility; repeated concurrent commands cannot dispatch a completed step or intent twice. An interrupted provider call can be explicitly retried with both attempts counted; inference is not exactly-once. Invalid output, exhausted limits, paused late responses, and restart preserve safe state and access. Existing plan tests remain; propose `tests/research-machine.test.ts` and API/restart cases under VER-01. Evidence includes transition snapshots, provider inputs/outputs/statuses, counters, ledger absence for negative cases, and restart traces.

**Scope / done:** in: bounded decisions, guards, checkpoints through COM, projections and safe resumption. Out: SQLite primitives, approval/settlement internals, automatic background scheduling, arbitrary plans. Done when each legal edge and rejection is exercised and no research path mutates spend/access.

## RES-03 — Actual baseline, cited final answer, and honest impact

**Priority: P0. Outcome / 80–20 reason:** prove what newly accessible evidence changed, including unchanged conclusions, using an actual saved baseline and inspectable claims.

**Current-code anchors:** `CANONICAL_THESIS`/`AFTER_*` and `buildClaims` prewrite outcomes by ID. `fixtureDossier` names particular sources and arbitrary first spans; `validateDossier` calls `validateClaimBindings(..., true)` even for live output, fabricating missing bindings. The validator checks span ownership but accepts extra uncited source IDs and provider family counts. `DossierSchema` defaults absent spans to `[]`; conclusion support is unchecked. Existing dossier tests cover two ownership cases only; streamed JSON is emitted before validation.

**Dependencies:** RES-01 span/provenance contract, RES-02 evidence checkpoints, COM-04 verified access; UI-02 renders impact; VER-01 owns independent semantic adjudication.

**Ordered subtasks:**

- [ ] RES-03.a Persist the validated pre-purchase answer, evidence manifest, config hash, revision, provider/model/status, and unresolved facets. No supported baseline means no purchase proposal; lack of readable relevant evidence saves an explicit abstention artifact and cannot authorize buying. Never reconstruct “before” from constants or let the provider overwrite it.
- [ ] RES-03.b Define bounded answer output: atomic claims, stance, materiality, owner-tuple citations, exact supporting excerpts, uncertainties, and conclusion claim references. Keep one assertion per material claim; stance is relative to the answer's stated thesis, not a publisher label.
- [ ] RES-03.c Validate strict schema, unique claims, nonempty material support, source/span ownership and current grant, excerpt substring, complete cited-source coverage, and conclusion references. Compute family counts from actually cited provenance roots. Reject missing/fabricated/foreign-version citations in every mode; remove first-span repair.
- [ ] RES-03.d Reassess after each verified delivery using only newly accessible spans plus the saved evidence set. Store per-purchase comparison links: baseline/current answer IDs, receipt/grant references, new spans, affected claim IDs, and impact `STRENGTHENS | QUALIFIES | CONTRADICTS | UNCHANGED`, with cited explanation. No-purchase final records `NO_PURCHASE`.
- [ ] RES-03.e Replace fallback prose with extractive accessible-span summaries and explicit uncertainty. Apply identical validators; fallback never reads private evaluation labels. Persist only complete validated answers. Mark stream deltas provisional; failed drafts never become ready. Keep prior valid artifact available with its revision and failure status.

**Failure and validation decisions:** structural validity is necessary, not a semantic correctness certificate. Private rubrics specify allowed meaning, scope/time qualifications, and prohibited overclaims for each span. VER-01 checks material claims and conclusion against those rubrics and records human review for live prose; claiming “all projects are delayed” from one regional operator fails despite valid citations. Expose `BINDINGS_VALIDATED`. Support disagreements remain uncertainty; metadata previews cannot support material final claims. Contradictory paid content must qualify/reverse the affected claim, never preserve the hardcoded `AFTER_MERIDIAN` answer. Unchanged wording can have stronger support; `UNCHANGED` may also mean no useful new support. Impact records support delta separately and never infer value from spend, text difference, or successful payment.

Provider errors retain failure category (HTTP, timeout, malformed JSON, schema, binding), usage, request correlation, and actual model where supplied. A failed live attempt is visibly blocked with its reason and cannot satisfy live rehearsal evidence. An explicitly selected fresh fixture run records fallback provenance; its extractive answer must pass the same validators or abstain. Logs redact credentials and inaccessible content; no silent settlement-mode change.

**Acceptance / verification:** baseline survives purchases/reload unchanged; no-purchase final is honest; renamed/rekeyed corpus yields equivalent support; contradictory delivery changes claims; corroborating delivery can retain conclusion while adding support. Missing spans, valid spans from another owner/version, gratuitous sources, unsupported material assertions, conclusion without claim references, and empty provider output fail. Extend existing `tests/dossier-validation.test.ts`; propose `tests/answer-impact.test.ts` and controlled-provider cases through VER-01. Record baseline/final manifests, exact citation resolutions, impact/receipt joins, rejected drafts, and curated semantic review. `npm run verify` exists; new test files/scenario commands remain proposed until implemented.

**Scope / done:** in: evidence packets, answer validation, baseline/impact storage through COM, labelled provider/fallback handling. Out: generic entailment models, financial recommendations, free-web citations, guaranteed positive purchase impact. Done when structural negatives and independent semantic rubric pass, and one live-model run demonstrates cited impact with local publisher/simulated settlement.

## Research oracle handoff and integration

Curated expectations; never runtime purchase branches:

| Variant | Required observation |
| --- | --- |
| Canonical unresolved grid | Baseline distinguishes announced demand from energisation; eligible original grid reporting may be proposed; no settlement before exact approval. |
| Open sufficient replacement | Two independent open families cover selected facets; STOP, zero quotes/intents/spend. |
| Rekey/rename and derivative duplicate | Equivalent decisions/support after tuple remapping; duplicate adds no independent family or purchase value. |
| Price above ceiling/available budget | Candidate blocked, affordable alternative or limited STOP; never reuse prior approval. |
| Contradictory paid version | Cite delivered facts, qualify/reverse affected claim, preserve baseline. |
| Corroborating paid version | Same conclusion allowed; show new support or explicitly no useful support. |
| No accessible evidence / hostile provider | Abstention or explicit blocked/fallback; no fabricated citations or locked leakage. |

The resolved integration section in INTERFACES fixes profile mapping, run/reset lifecycle, citation tuples, delivery encoding and independent mode labels. Follow those decisions when replacing legacy `siteKey`, named thesis/impact fields and broad horizon editing. COM owns quote/money/grant wire payloads and checkpoint transactions; research adopts them without another persistence path. Existing canonical E2E assumptions requiring Northstar before Meridian and XRP-to-SGD conversions must be revised with their owners. Implementation evidence records commit, acceptance results, commands/exit statuses, and failure artifacts.
