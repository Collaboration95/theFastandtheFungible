05

Buy, then revise

Only after revalidation: reserve 90 c, settle, verify delivery, cite the series and qualify the conclusion.

Keep the financial boundariesEach worker: gross spent + reserved ≤ its ceiling.
Sum of worker ceilings ≤ the user’s 200 c ceiling.
Account commitments must also fit spendable funds after fees.
Do not rebalance money already committedNo stealing reservations, uncertain settlements or prior spend. Retain the per-purchase cap (100 c in this example). Preserve the current rule: refunds do not reopen the run’s gross budget.
Why this is a team, and where the authority belongs

The workers pursue different evidence goals and can disagree about which missing fact matters. They share acquired findings so a finished task can release authority. The controller validates requests and applies the allocation rule; the original research synthesizer still writes the final answer.

Favor expected improvement in the user’s requested facts, not whichever worker asks loudest. Reallocate only if the recipient still offers useful evidence after repricing and the donor has no higher-priority unresolved need. Keep reservations and limit revisions in one transaction; tag intents and receipts with worker and policy revision. If no safe donor exists, abstain and show the remaining gap.

Integration unknown: native Metal agent limits may exist, or limits may need application enforcement around one signer. Verify that boundary before presenting application bookkeeping as banking-provider enforcement. The supplied brief does not specify it.

04

Promising, with real caveats.

Editorial judgments, not measured scores. “Ethos” here means my interpretation of the supplied brief: meaningful agent decisions under financial constraints. These are not official judging criteria.

Coolness

A strong moment

Unused research allowance unlocks a cited fact while the budget stays fixed. Mere bar animation has little surprise.

Originality

Modest

Multi-agent budgets and paid APIs are familiar. Evidence quality, supplier promises and the free-search donor are the distinctive combination.

Practical usefulness

Plausible

Useful for diligence across specialist sources. Real sellers, rights, prices and customer demand are unvalidated; the corpus is fictional.

Agentic depth

Conditional

Strong if evidence gaps and alternatives drive transfers. Weak if every shock triggers the same scripted 30 c move.

Visual demo appeal

High potential

The existing paced trace and cited answer provide a good stage. Price → blocked buy → released limit → answer revision is easy to follow.

Feasibility

Split

Bounded team/UI work fits the codebase. Metal settlement, authorization and supplier compatibility are the critical unknowns; no live integration was tested.

Kit 11 fit / inferred ethos

Compelling if the account control is real

Three workers, one account and a supplier-cost increase match the supplied description directly. The agent decisions should concern evidence, not inference routing. An XRPL-only replay or application limits presented as native bank controls would leave the important fit unproven.

Team depth vs. extra machinery

Use a question with independent evidence goals. Do not turn retrieval, payment and writing into three “agents” just to meet the number.

Adaptation vs. price chasing

Higher prices reduce value per credit. Sometimes the right outcome is a cheaper source, free evidence or an explicit unknown—not a bigger allowance.

A reliable story vs. a rigged result

Script the supplier event for repeatability; label it. Let policy decide the response. Also show a refusal case when no safe slack exists.

05

Expand the buyer, keep the product.

Recommended direction: a small research team that reallocates spending authority to finish a cited diligence answer. Keep the current ResearchAgent screen and its five hard gates.

Reuse

The parts already doing useful work

  • Free-first retrieval, requested-fact coverage and focused follow-up search.
  • Value/price decisions, trust weighting and rewrite avoidance.
  • Intent identity, atomic reservation pattern, reconciliation and gated citations.
  • Plan confirmation, paced trace, answer revisions and audit details.
Highest-value additions

In this order

  1. Prove the Metal path. Narrow buyer → supplier → settlement → accessible content, under the intended one-account controls.
  2. Add account + worker policy. Attributed intents, atomic reservations and limit revisions; keep unknown settlements committed.
  3. Add three goal-driven workers. One parent run, shared grants, one synthesis. Reprice and compare before reallocating.
  4. Make the causal demo. Price shock, donor reason, invariant, answer delta and a frozen-limit comparison.
Unresolved dependencies

Do not hand-wave these

  • Metal specifics: network/asset, x402 scheme, signer, funding, fees, finality, receipt verification and refund support are unspecified here.
  • Enforcement: native delegated limits vs. application policy; how one account is represented and authorized.
  • Both ends of payment: current facilitators and manifest wallet verification use XRPL keys, not a generic Metal adapter.
  • Evidence economy: real supplier access, purchase rights and relevance of repeated repricing remain unvalidated.
The next decision

Validate the rail before polishing the team.

Proceed with Kit 11 if the intended Metal wallet and account-control boundary can support a narrow verified purchase. Then build the smallest team that demonstrates one useful reallocation.

If that path is unavailable, the visual concept is still good, but the retrofit is a weak completed Kit 11 entry. Keep it labelled as a concept. Do not spend the effort building a generic inference router or a separate finance dashboard.

The payment migration is broader than changing a network string

shared/contracts/publisher.ts and shared/contracts/x402.ts constrain the rail, asset and network to simulated/XRPL. server/purchases.ts explicitly depends on XrplPayer for ledger reconciliation; publisher/facilitator.ts settles the matching scheme. shared/manifest.ts verifies signatures against XRPL-derived wallet addresses. Receipts, refund handling and UI ledger types also carry XRPL assumptions. [E3] [E6]

Reuse the protocol boundaries and safety invariants. Revalidate the signing, settlement and manifest-identity implementation for Metal. The supplied setup does not establish what SDK, token, fee model or delegated-limit API is available. No outside documentation was used to fill those gaps.

Open the source ledger · 8 compact evidence groups
  1. Retrieval and coverage · retrieve / followUpSearch · requested facts and coverage

    Federated public hits, free reads, signed-manifest rejection, accessible-evidence checks and bounded follow-up are present. This is more than a README promise.

  2. Buying decisions · decideRound · price/value tests

    Price affects value-per-dollar and eligibility; trust cannot raise caps. The UC3 tests compare fixed source prices and quarantine behavior, not a mid-run supplier price shock or a team reallocation.

  3. Purchases and payment rails · PurchaseManager · facilitator · rail / asset contracts · XRPL tests

    Buyer/facilitator quote binding, once-only submission, restart reconciliation and refunds are implemented. Tests inject a ledger double; their existence is not evidence of a Metal deployment or a live ledger run in this evaluation.

  4. Financial policy · reserveIntent / claimSubmitting · budget / run contracts · competing intents / price mismatch tests

    Commitments are selected with WHERE run_id=?. A quote/evaluated-price mismatch is skipped without charging. No account-wide agent-limit ledger or automatic repricing/rebalancing was found.

  5. The existing experience · layout · plan confirmation · trace pacing

    Reuse the run/answer/budget presentation. The 5-second modal confirms the plan; the budget authorizes spending. There is no per-purchase approval modal to invent or preserve.

  6. Delivery integrity and quarantine · signature / claim-pattern checks · proof gate · quarantine / content leakage tests

    Checks establish delivery against signed promises. They do not fact-check the world or make a second research agent redundant.

  7. Talk and publishing route · talk at inspected gh-pages commit · manual page-copy publishing script · main CI

    The presentation is on a separate branch and includes static traces. Existing hosting serves gh-pages:/; the publishing script copies HTML and relies on a push to redeploy. Main CI checks code; it does not publish PR previews.

  8. Storyboard source facts · margin analysis · lead-time series · free solar evidence · story bible

    All are synthetic. Numbers and source prices were checked in the article JSON. The combined question, team, transfer and 40 → 90 c price event are new illustrative proposals.
