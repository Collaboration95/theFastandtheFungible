Wealth Management Intelligence
Presented by Wikiped from Bank Julius Baer, this challenge focuses on creating an intelligence layer for Relationship Managers (RMs).
*   Objective: Develop an AI-augmented tool to help RMs answer three core questions: What does a client's portfolio look like? Why is it performing that way? What should be done next?
*   Technical Scope: Participants can use any tech stack but must utilize provided datasets on GitHub. The goal is human-in-the-loop augmentation rather than full automation.

Agentic Payments
Presented by Tats, this challenge centers on the evolution of payment systems using advanced AI agents.
*   Focus: Utilizing the XRP Ledger, stablecoins such as the Ripple USD stablecoin, and agentic payment frameworks.
*   Collaboration: Strategic partner company T54 will be available to provide guidance on building within this ecosystem.
