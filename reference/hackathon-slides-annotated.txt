SLIDE 02
Source image: IMG_7187.jpeg
Visible slide/page marker: none visible
Footer: SingHacks 2026 - Julius Baer Challenge
Brand/footer mark: Julius Bär

Title

From Portfolio Monitoring to Intelligence

CHALLENGE

Design and prototype an AI-powered wealth intelligence layer that turns
portfolio data into advice, taking the relationship manager from “what does my
client’s portfolio look like?” to “what should I know, and what should I do
next?” Preserve the RM’s central role; address suitability and explainability.
*No restrictions on the technology stack.

3 BUILDING BLOCKS

1. Intelligent Portfolio Explanations

Explain what a portfolio did and why, connecting real market and geopolitical
events to the individual holdings that moved

2. Proactive Risk and Opportunity Detection

Surface concentration, liquidity, currency and mandate risks, and event-driven
ideas, before the client has to ask

3. RM Intelligence Workbench

Turn insight into client-ready actions: rebalancing, tax-aware optimisation,
stress testing and life-event planning

JUDGING CRITERIA

- Client-Centric Innovation
- User Experience and Design
- Technical and Operational Feasibility
- Strategic Impact

Remember: How you present the prototype will impact your scoring

Visual/layout annotation

The challenge appears in a bordered horizontal box near the top. The three
building blocks appear as stacked shaded rows with icons on the left. Judging
criteria appear in a separate right-hand column, with a dashed “Remember”
callout below.

Capture artifacts

A video-call participant tile, sharing controls, and room imagery appear around
the slide and are not slide content.
