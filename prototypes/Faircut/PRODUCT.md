# FairCut product brief

## Problem

Leah Tan needs one tense-but-hopeful 12-second cue for a 20-second Japan travel
film before midnight. A traditional checkout can sell a file, but it does not
compare creative fit inside her cut or prove that the exact narrow licence,
payee, and delivered bytes agree.

## People

- **Leah Tan:** Singapore-based independent filmmaker; sets the commercial
  social mandate for Singapore and Japan, six months, 12 seconds, provenance
  required, and a 10,000-drop ceiling.
- **Mika Reyes:** creator of the best eligible cue; receives the exact 8,000-drop
  Testnet payment at a provider-controlled destination.
- **FairCut edit agent:** searches and auditions; it can recommend, but the
  deterministic guard decides rights and the server-only signer decides whether
  the frozen intent is signable.

## Journey

1. Leah opens a populated rough cut with the unresolved 12-second placement.
2. FairCut compares three offers across independent provider identities inside
   the cut.
3. The strongest-sounding Neon Pilgrim preview is blocked before signing for
   commercial-use, Japan-territory, and creator/payee-binding failures.
4. Leah selects Dawn Current from two eligible options and reviews the exact
   8,000-drop package.
5. FairCut settles one bounded payment, verifies the clean bytes and licence,
   replaces the preview, and opens a receipt separating payment from rights and
   delivery claims.

The failure rehearsals make changed quotes, provider/risk outages, ambiguous
ledger state, delivery mismatch, and replay safe and visible.
