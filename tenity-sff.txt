Singapore FinTech Festival 2025 and Tenity Review

The main lesson is:

Tenity did not rely on one booth or one demo. It built a funnel from talent -> prototypes -> partner validation -> SFF stage exposure -> investor and market-access conversations.

What Tenity did well

1. Created a strong, specific wedge

SingHacks was positioned as Asia's first fintech-focused agentic-AI hackathon. It used real challenge owners—Julius Baer, Hedera, Ancileo and MSIG—rather than generic "build anything" prompts. Tenity reported 270+ participants, 30+ partners, multiple prototypes and finalists progressing to the SFF Catalyst stage.

Source: https://www.tenity.com/cases/singhacks/

2. Connected the pre-event to SFF

The hackathon ran from 31 October–2 November, then finalists pitched at SFF on 14 November. That gave Tenity a multi-week narrative instead of a single event moment.

Source: https://www.tenity.com/tenity-concludes-singhacks-asias-first-fintech-focused-hackathon-for-agentic-ai/

3. Used cross-border market access as a second story

Tenity and KB Financial Group hosted a curated Korea–Singapore startup showcase, with six Korean startups, an Enterprise Singapore representative, a Korea SMEs & Startups Agency representative, and a panel on cross-border growth.

Source: https://luma.com/8lq00ste

4. Plugged into high-intent investor activity

Tenity supported Investor Hours, which included 1:1 investor pitch tables, masterclasses, portfolio showcases and networking. They marketed it using the previous year's baseline of 582 meetings, 307 startups and 161 investors.

Source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/tenity_singapore-fintech-festival-2025-investor-activity-7377220401191481344-G_dF

5. Maintained an official physical presence

Tenity had booth 4L25 and positioned itself as both a corporate innovation partner and a dedicated startup accelerator—not merely as an event organiser.

Source: https://www.fintechfestival.sg/2025-exhibitors/tenity

SFF itself was extremely crowded: more than 70,000 attendees from 142 countries, 900 speakers and approximately 300 sessions.

Source: https://www.fintechfestival.sg/media-hub/latest-news/wraps-up-10th-edition

Where our team should improve

For the current Agent Spend Guard / Ripple direction, prioritise these areas:

- Positioning: Use one sharp sentence: "An AI agent can purchase a useful digital service, but cannot exceed its delegated budget or change the approved payee."

- Commercial realism: Show a complete loop: customer need -> agent discovery -> decision -> policy check -> XRPL payment -> fulfilled service -> receipt.

- Trust and safety: Make the blocked over-budget path a central demo moment, not a footnote. Also show approval for a new or insufficiently verified merchant.

- Technical proof: Demonstrate one real XRPL Testnet transaction, explorer link, payment status, fulfilment evidence and idempotency handling.

- Pitch discipline: Build around one customer, one service, one payment and one outcome. Avoid presenting every protocol or future integration.

- Partner value: Prepare a concrete pilot proposal for Ripple, t54 or Unlimit: target user, integration required, 30-day experiment and success metric.

- Meeting strategy: Go into SFF with a target list and pre-booked conversations. Aim for feedback, a pilot conversation or a partner introduction—not general networking.

- Evidence: Keep one canonical scorecard: demo success rate, payment success, fulfilment success, blocked-risk cases, time saved and pilot interest. Tenity's public materials use different solution counts—60 projects, 30+ enterprise-ready solutions and later 90+ solutions—so our team should be precise about measurement.

- Follow-through: Define what happens 7, 30 and 90 days after the event. The public Tenity material strongly communicates participation and prototype volume, but I did not find named post-event pilot results. This is an opportunity for our team to look more outcome-driven.

How to align with SFF 2026

SFF 2026 runs from 18–20 November and is framed around "The Coming Rewiring of the Financial System: New Risks. New Rules. New Growth," across technology, policy, geoeconomics, capital and talent.

Source: https://www.fintechfestival.sg/join-sff2026

Our strongest alignment is:

- Technology: agentic payments and XRPL settlement
- Policy: delegated authority, risk controls and auditability
- Capital: machine-to-machine commerce and programmable spending

Tenity's own 2026 SingHacks positioning has also become sharper: fewer, more concrete challenge areas, stronger anchor sponsors, and direct visibility with the companies behind the challenges.

Source: https://www.singhacks.com/

Recommendation

Spend the next iteration on reliability, proof and narrative coherence before adding features. Our winning advantage is not "we connected many protocols"; it is "we showed an agent making an economically useful decision safely."
