02The release rail

Every stop has a receipt.

4 milestones and 13 releases. Open a stop for what shipped, a screenshot or terminal output, and the proof: the pull request, the release notes or a Testnet transaction.

  1. September · SingHacks

    1. 19–20 SepMilestoneSingHacks: the prototype

      The prototype that took us to the finals: one web page, synthetic sources, a language model proposing what to buy, and a person approving every quote.

      What shipped

      • A research desk that bought synthetic premium sources within an XRP budget and wrote a cited dossier.
      • A language model proposed each purchase and a person approved every quote before payment (manual approval was required in the event build); payment was a fixture by default, with optional Testnet settlement.
      • The frozen prototype held 34 files and 970 lines of code (tag hackathon-baseline).

      Prototype tag↗

  2. October · AI Tinkerers build

    1. 5 OctMilestoneFirst payment on the XRPL Testnet

      Writers were paid at their own Testnet wallets, and the run showed the ledger entry.

      What shipped

      • Each writer gets its own Testnet wallet and is paid there directly.
      • The run shows the ledger transaction instead of a simulated receipt.
      • Testnet money has no real value; that label has stayed on every surface since.

      Pull request #101↗Wallet per writer #103↗

    2. 8 OctMilestoneCode decides what to buy, inside your budget

      Purchases moved from the language model to deterministic policy code, inside a budget set once.

      What shipped

      • A neutral search across every listed writer instead of an allowlist of sites.
      • A decision model scores each article; only policy code can make the purchase.
      • A five-second plan card confirms the plan; no pop-up interrupts each purchase.

      Checkpoint tag↗Design record↗

    3. 10 OctMilestoneAI Tinkerers Singapore talk

      An on-stage demo of the October build for AI Tinkerers Singapore, a separate chapter from the finals.

      What shipped

      • The refund story on stage: a bought article breaks its promise and the money comes back.
      • The October build log, replayed from git and the coding agents' logs, is kept as an archive.
      • The talk deck and speaker notes stay on this site.

      October build log↗Talk slides↗
